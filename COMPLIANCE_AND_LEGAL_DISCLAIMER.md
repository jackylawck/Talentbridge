# ⚖️ Compliance, Governance & Legal Declaration 
# 法律、合規與 AI 治理聲明

## 1. Nature of the Software / 軟件性質界定
**[EN]** This project (`TalentBridge HR AI Governance Engine`) is a deterministic, rule-based compliance gateway and Privacy-Enhancing Technology (PET). **It does not contain, train, or operate any Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), or Generative AI models natively.** Its primary function is to act as a governance guardrail (data minimization and risk scoring) for enterprise systems that integrate with external AI APIs.
**[CN]** 本項目（滬港賢智匯 AI 治理引擎）屬於確定性、基於規則的合規網關與隱私增強技術（PET）。**本項目本身不包含、不訓練、亦不運行任何人工智能（AI）、機器學習（ML）或生成式 AI 模型。** 其核心定位為企業在對接外部 AI 系統時的「治理護欄」，提供數據最小化脫敏與風險計分功能。

## 2. Applicability of Global Frameworks / 全球合規框架適用性映射

To prevent over-engineering while maintaining strict governance standards, the applicability of global regulations to this specific tool is classified as follows:
為避免過度承諾並維持嚴謹的治理標準，本工具對全球法規的適用性界定如下：

### 🟢 Highly Applicable (Direct Implementation) / 高度適用（直接落實）
* **Hong Kong PDPO (香港《個人資料(私隱)條例》) & Mainland China PIPL (內地《個人信息保護法》):** 
  * *Implementation:* The `HRDataDeidentifier` module directly enforces the **Data Minimization Principle** (PDPO DPP1(1) & PIPL Art. 6) by pre-scrubbing PII (HKID, CN ID, etc.) before data leaves the enterprise boundary.
* **ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System) & ISO/IEC 27701 (Privacy Information Management):**
  * *Implementation:* The `AIRiskScorer` acts as a technical control for AI Impact Assessments (AIIA), aligning with ISO 42001 Annex A.5 (Risk Assessment) and ISO 27701 Privacy-by-Design requirements.

### 🟡 Indirectly Applicable (Mitigation Enabler) / 間接適用（作為緩釋工具）
* **EU Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act / 歐盟 AI 法案):**
  * *Clarification:* This tool is **NOT** classified as an "AI System" under Article 3 of the EU AI Act. However, because HR and recruitment AI systems are classified as **High-Risk (Annex III)**, this tool serves as a mandatory *Risk Management System* and *Data Governance* mitigation measure (Articles 9 & 10) for deployers targeting the EU market.
  * *澄清：* 本工具不屬於歐盟 AI 法案界定的「AI 系統」。但由於 HR 招聘 AI 屬法案附件三之「高風險 AI」，本工具可作為部署者履行法案第 9 條（風險管理）與第 10 條（數據治理）的關鍵技術緩釋手段。
* **CAC Generative AI Measures (國家網信辦《生成式人工智能服務管理暫行辦法》):**
  * *Clarification:* This tool is not a public-facing GenAI service. It is an internal enterprise control mechanism to help organizations fulfill the CAC's requirements for preventing algorithmic discrimination (Art. 4) and protecting personal information (Art. 11).

### 🔴 Not Applicable / 不適用
* **ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security Management):** While this tool supports secure data handling, ISO 27001 certifies entire organizational information security management systems (ISMS), not standalone open-source code repositories.
* **Model Training Regulations (e.g., Copyright directives for web scraping):** This tool does not scrape data or train models.
